# Tables, figures, formulas, references, notes, footnotes (п. 5.4–5.9)

## 5.4 Формулы

- Continuous Arabic numbering; the number sits **at the formula's level, right, in
  parentheses at the end of the line** (п. 5.4.1). A single formula is `(1)`.
- At least **one blank line** above and below every formula/equation.
- In-text references to a formula number go in parentheses: «в формуле (1)».
- Formulas in appendices are numbered with the appendix letter: `(В.1)`.
- Numbering **within a section** is allowed: section number + index through a dot — `(3.1)`
  (п. 5.4.2).
- **Symbol explanations** directly under the formula: each symbol on its own line in the order
  of the formula; the first line starts with **«где»** (no colon) **with the paragraph
  indent** (п. 5.4.3).

```
Плотность каждого образца, кг/м3, вычисляют по формуле

   ρ = m/v = 12/3 = 4 кг/м3                                  (1)

где m – масса образца, кг;
    v – объем образца, м3.
```

- Formulas following each other without text between are separated by a **comma**:

```
S = V ∙ t ,                                                    (1)

   ρ = m/v                                                     (2)
```

- A formula is wrapped only at operation signs, repeating the sign at the start of the next
  line; when wrapping at multiplication use «×».
- Equations are treated like formulas. Formulas must be typed **with an equation editor**
  (scans are not allowed).

## 5.5 Таблицы

- Tables present numerical material for clarity and comparison (п. 5.5.1).
- The **caption goes above** the table **with the paragraph indent**; no dot after the
  number, then a **dash** and the caption: `Таблица N − Название` (п. 5.5.2).
- The table itself: **from the left edge** (no indent), **page-wide**. **One blank line after
  the caption.**
- When a table is continued, the caption is placed only over the first part.
- Continuous Arabic numbering (п. 5.5.3); appendix tables — `Таблица А.1`; a single table —
  «Таблица 1»; numbering within a section — `Таблица 2.1`.
- Every table is referenced in the text with the word «таблица» and its number.
- Column/row headers with a capital; subheaders lowercase when they form one sentence with the
  header, else capital; **no dots** at the end; singular (п. 5.5.4). A smaller font than the
  body text is allowed.
- Diagonal lines splitting headers/titles are not allowed (п. 5.5.5); headers are written
  parallel to the rows (perpendicular only if necessary, never alternating).
- The table goes under the first reference to it or on the next page, or in an appendix; it may
  be placed along the long side of the sheet (п. 5.5.6).
- If rows do not fit, the table is split into parts, repeating the header/sidebar in each part:
  the word «Таблица» appears **once** over the first part, and over the other parts in italics
  «Продолжение таблицы N». When a table breaks at a page end, the bottom horizontal line of the
  first part is not drawn.
- The **«Номер по порядку» column is not allowed** (п. 5.5.7); row numbers live in the first
  (sidebar) column before the name. Letter designations per ГОСТ 2.321: D – diameter, H –
  height, L – length.
- Tables are separated from the text by **one blank line**.

## 5.6 Иллюстрации

- Graphic material is called **«рисунок»**; the caption may say схема, диаграмма, график
  (п. 5.6.1).
- Placed directly after the first mention or on the next page, or in an appendix; per ЕСКД,
  computer-made and coloured is fine (п. 5.6.2); always referenced in the text.
- The **caption goes below** the figure, **centred**: `Рисунок 1 − Детали прибора`
  (п. 5.6.3). **Explanatory data** go between the figure and the caption.
- References: «в соответствии с рисунком 2» or «…рисунком 1.2» (within a section).
- Continuous numbering (п. 5.6.4): `Рисунок 1`, within a section `Рисунок 1.1`, appendix
  `Рисунок А.3`.
- A figure normally fits one page (п. 5.6.5). When carried over: caption on the first page,
  explanatory data on each, with «Рисунок …, лист …» below.
- Units on diagrams (п. 5.6.6): at the end of the scale between the last and second-to-last
  number; or after the variable name; or as a fraction at the end of the scale. Angle units are
  written once at the last scale number.

## 5.7 Ссылки

- References are allowed to RK standards, legislative/normative acts and special literature
  (п. 5.7).
- They are given by the source's number in the bibliography, in square brackets, per
  ГОСТ 7.1-2003: `[1]`, `[2]`.

## 5.8 Примечания

- Explanatory/reference notes to text, tables or graphic material; they must not contain
  requirements (п. 5.8.1).
- Placed directly after the element they refer to; printed with a capital letter with the
  paragraph indent, not underlined (п. 5.8.2).
- A single note: **«Примечание –»** (word + dash), not numbered. A note to a table goes at the
  table's end, above the closing line. Several notes are numbered: «Примечания: 1 …; 2 …».

## 5.9 Сноски

- Footnote signs explain individual data (п. 5.9.1).
- Footnotes go at the page bottom with the paragraph indent, separated by a short thin rule on
  the left; in a table — at the table's end, above the line.
- The sign is placed directly after the word/number it explains and before the note text
  (п. 5.9.2).
- Arabic digits with or without a bracket, at the top of the font's height: `… Интернет2) …`
  (п. 5.9.3). Numbering is separate for each page. Asterisks are allowed (max four).
- Footnotes must not be carried to the next page (tables excepted).

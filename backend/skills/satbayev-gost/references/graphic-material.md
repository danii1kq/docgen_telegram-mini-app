## 6.2 Inscriptions, technical requirements and tables

(ГОСТ 2.316-2008; title block — ГОСТ 2.104-2006.) A drawing may contain a text part
(technical requirements and/or characteristics), labels for images and elements, and tables
with dimensions, parameters, requirements.

- The drawing should be filled **90 % by area, 75 % by density**.
- Title block per **ГОСТ 2.104**; text and labels concise and accurate.
- Parameter tables (e.g. a gear) per **ГОСТ 2.316**; other tables on the free field right/below
  the image per **ГОСТ 2.105-95**; numbered within the drawing when referenced; the word
  «Таблица» on the left (no «№»); a single table is not numbered.
- Technical requirements are grouped by homogeneity; the technical characteristic goes
  separately under the heading «Техническая характеристика»; above the requirements —
  «Технические требования». Neither heading is underlined.

## 6.3 Scales (ГОСТ 2.302-68)

Reduction: 1:2; 1:2,5; 1:4; 1:5; 1:10; 1:15; 1:20; 1:25; 1:40; 1:50; 1:75; 1:100; 1:200;
1:400; 1:500; 1:800; 1:1000. Natural: 1:1. Enlargement: 2:1; 2,5:1; 4:1; 5:1; 10:1; 20:1;
40:1; 50:1; 100:1.

- General plans of large objects: 1:1000 … 1:50000.
- Enlargement (100ⁿ):1 with integer n is allowed where necessary.
- Geological/survey drawings additionally 1:100000 … 1:500000.
- In the title block the scale is written `1:1`, `1:2`; elsewhere `М1:1`, `М1:2`, `М2:1`.

## 6.4 Lines (ГОСТ 2.303-68)

Line styles per **ГОСТ 2.303-68** (solid thick main, solid thin, dashed, dash-dotted, etc.).

**Related standards:** ГОСТ 2.004-88 (printer/graphic devices), ГОСТ 2.106-96 (text
documents, specifications), ГОСТ 2.304-81 (drafting fonts), ГОСТ 2.321-84 (letter
designations).

## 6.8 Demonstration materials (posters)

- For the defence of dissertations and projects, illustrative material is recommended as
  **demonstration posters** per **ГОСТ 2.605**.
- Each poster: a **heading**, the **image part**, explanatory text if needed. The heading goes
  in the **upper middle part**; explanatory text on a free field.
- Names and texts must match the explanatory note.
- **Frame** — **20 mm** from the sheet edge around the perimeter. When made without scale the
  title block and additional columns are not drawn; for ЕСКД drawings the title block is
  mandatory (ГОСТ 2.104-2006).
- Colour printing is allowed; inscriptions in a drafting font (ГОСТ 2.304-81) or standard
  computer fonts.
- Recommended in the lower right: work type and theme, author, group index, chair name.

## 6.9 Transparent-film materials

- Ready materials on transparent film — A4 (210×294); drawings may be made on A3 (297×420)
  and reduced.
- Demonstration posters — as in 6.8. The defence may use multimedia presentations.

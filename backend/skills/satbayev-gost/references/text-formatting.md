# Text formatting (section 5.1–5.3 of СТ КазНИТУ 09-2023)

## 5.1 How works are executed

| Parameter | Value | Clause |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Sheet | A4 210×297 mm, **one side** | 5.1.2 |
| Large tables/figures | A3 (297×420) and A2 (420×594) allowed | 5.1.2 |
| Editor | Microsoft Word | 5.1.4 |
| Font | **Times New Roman** | 5.1.4 |
| Body text size | **14** («через один интервал») | 5.1.4 |
| Title page and annotation | **12** | 5.1.4 |
| Table header | 12 allowed | 5.1.4 |
| Line spacing | **single** | 5.1.4 |
| Paragraph indent | uniform, five characters (~1.25 cm) | 5.1.4 |
| Text density | uniform | 5.1.4 |

**Margins (п. 5.1.3):** top 20 mm, bottom 25 mm, left 30 mm, right **10 mm** —
for a master's dissertation the right margin is **15 mm**. Text pages are **not** framed
(exception: specifications per ГОСТ 2.106-96).

**Language (п. 5.1.1):** Kazakh or Russian per the Law «О языках в Республике Казахстан».

**Handwritten inserts (п. 5.1.5):** words, formulas, symbols, Latin/Greek letters, drawings —
black ink; density close to the printed text. **Corrections (п. 5.1.6):** typos may be
corrected in place; no more than five corrections per page; damaged sheets or traces of
removed text are not allowed. **Specifications (п. 5.1.7):** drafting font, pencil or ink.

### Page numbering (п. 5.1.8)

- Continuous numbering of all sheets, starting from the title page, including appendices
  and specifications.
- Numbers are printed **from the page with «ВВЕДЕНИЕ»** — Arabic, **centred at the bottom,
  no dot**.
- The title page counts in the sequence but shows no number.

## 5.2 Dividing the text

- Text is divided into **sections**; sections into items or subsections and items; items into
  subitems (п. 5.2.1). Each item must carry complete information.
- Arabic numbering, written **with the paragraph indent** (п. 5.2.2): section `1`, `2`;
  subsection/item `1.1`, `1.2`; subitem `1.1.1.1`.
- **Every section** of a diploma work (project) starts on a **new page** (п. 5.2.2).
- **No dot after the number** of a section/subsection/item/subitem (п. 5.2.2).
- A section/subsection with a single item (or an item with a single subitem) is not numbered.

### Headings (п. 5.2.3)

- Sections and subsections **must have headings**; items usually have none.
- A heading starts **with the paragraph indent**, with a **capital** letter, **no dot** at the
  end, **not underlined**.
- Two sentences in a heading are separated by a dot.
- Spacing: one line between a section heading and a subsection heading; two lines between
  subsections of the same section (after the text, before the next subsection).

```
1 Аналитический обзор. Современные тенденции   ← section heading
                                                ← one line
1.1 Аппараты, материалы, реактивы               ← subsection heading
                                                ← one line
Текст ……
                                                ← two lines
1.2 Анализ результатов …
```

### Lists (п. 5.2.3)

- A **dash** precedes every list item.
- When the text refers to an item — a **lowercase Russian letter + bracket, no dot**: `а) …`.
- For a deeper level — **Arabic digits + bracket, no dot**, written with **two paragraph
  indents**: `1) …`.

```
Неуравновешенность может возникнуть в результате:
– несбалансированности вращающихся элементов;
– лёгкой деформации рамы;
– аэродинамических нагрузок.
```

## 5.3 Wording requirements

- Text must be **concise and unambiguous**; use terms and abbreviations established by
  standards or laws (п. 5.3.1).
- **Roman numerals** only for grades/classes of a product, chemical valence, quarters and
  half-years; otherwise Arabic digits (п. 5.3.2).
- The unit of a physical quantity must stay **the same** throughout; units per
  **ГОСТ 8.417-2002** (п. 5.3.3).

**Not allowed (п. 5.3.4):** synonyms for one notion, foreign words when equivalent Kazakh or
Russian words exist; abbreviations beyond orthography/standards; invented word forms;
unit symbols without digits (except table headers/sidebars and symbol lists in formulas).

**Not allowed outside formulas/tables/figures (п. 5.3.5):** the minus sign before negative
values (write «минус»); the ⌀ sign for diameter (write «диаметр»; ⌀ only on figures before a
dimension number); mathematical signs without values (>, <, =, ≥, ≤, ≠, №, %) — write
«больше», «меньше», «равно», «номер», «процент»; a standard's index without its designation,
or splitting index and designation across lines/pages.

**Practical rule for generated text:** «минус 5 °C», «диаметр 20», «больше 10» — not
«−5 °C», «⌀20», «>10».
